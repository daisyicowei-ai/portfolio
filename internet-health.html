Internet Health: Data Visualisations

Can internet shutdowns ever be justified? A D3.js exploration.

As part of the Internet Health module, our team explored the ethics and impact of government-imposed internet blackouts. We focused on Myanmar and India, regions with frequent interruptions tied to political unrest.

01 How do countries justify internet shutdowns?

02 Who orders these shutdowns?

03 What are the emotional impacts on affected people?

01 · My contribution

Making the data explorable and human.

  • Clearly articulated the research questions behind the data.
  • Coded an interactive D3.js visualization showing shutdown frequency, duration, and regional hotspots.
  • Enabled users to filter over time and by region, with hover tooltips for context and emotion.
  • Designed the layout to bring quantitative trends and human stories together.
